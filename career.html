Career Tips

Tips to Guide Your Journey

Small habits that make a big difference on your path to a STEM career.

Start with small goals

Break your career path into small, achievable milestones instead of trying to plan everything at once.

Find a mentor

Talking to someone already in the field can save you years of trial and error.

Document your work

Keep a portfolio of every project. Employers care more about what you've built than what you've read.

Stay curious and adapt

STEM fields evolve fast. Keep learning new tools and don't be afraid to shift direction.

Apply for real opportunities

Use STEM Connect's Opportunities page to find internships, hackathons, and scholarships early on.

It's okay to change paths

Many professionals switch careers. What matters is building transferable skills along the way.
